# FOLS media library

Add approved church media to the folders below. Keep filenames lowercase, use hyphens instead of spaces, and avoid replacing the existing logo assets in `public/brand`.

## Folder structure

- `hero/` — homepage and page-hero background videos or poster images
- `videos/` — approved non-hero videos used elsewhere on the public website
- `images/` — general worship, church, outreach, campus, and congregation photography
- `ministries/` — Children's Church, Teens, Unstoppables, and Family & Marriage media
- `leadership/` — approved leadership portraits only
- `events/` — event artwork and photography
- `sermons/` — local sermon poster images only; sermon videos remain on YouTube
- `placeholders/` — reserved for temporary non-production assets

## Recommended hero files

The site is ready for these preferred names:

- `hero/home-hero.mp4` — 1920×1080 or larger, H.264 MP4, muted, 8–20 seconds, seamless loop
- `hero/home-hero-poster.webp` — 1920×1080 WebP poster/fallback
- `hero/about-hero.webp`
- `hero/ministries-hero.webp`
- `hero/visit-hero.webp`
- `ministries/unstoppables-hero.webp`

Keep individual hero videos as small as practical, ideally below 12 MB. Supply WebP or AVIF for photographs and retain originals outside the deployed `public` folder.

Do not add copyrighted, unapproved, private, or identifiable member media without the necessary church approval and consent.
